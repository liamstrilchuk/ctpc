About CTPC

We're a student-led organization making coding and building opportunities accessible, welcoming, and exciting.

Who we are

CTPC (the Canadian Team Programming Competition organization) is a national, student-led organization. We started by running the Canadian Team Programming Competition itself, and have since grown into the parent organization behind a number of events for students — including #Launch.

Our mission

Our mission is to make coding and building opportunities accessible, welcoming, and exciting for students — including those who are completely new to tech. We believe you shouldn't need years of experience or a head start to feel like you belong in this space.

What we run

We started with programming competitions, and we've grown from there. Today we run different kinds of events where students can solve problems, meet peers, and build confidence — from the Canadian Team Programming Competition to #Launch, our newer event built around hands-on building rather than a traditional contest format.

Our biggest event to date, the CTPC competition, brought together participants from across Canada — you can see it on our timeline.

Get involved

Students can enter the current competition or explore what participating felt like on our platform showcase. Schools, volunteers, and sponsors interested in partnering with us can reach out through our contact page — we'd love to hear from you.
